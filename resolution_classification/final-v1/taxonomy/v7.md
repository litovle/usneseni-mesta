# EleCzech resolution taxonomy v7

Classify the primary substantive domain, not the legal or financing mechanism.

## `property`

Municipal real estate/property rights and generic asset management: sale, purchase, lease, loan, housing allocation, easements, disposal. Domain-specific facility operations may follow the domain instead.

## `finance`

Municipal budget, accounting, funds, financial results, receivables/claims, banking and city-level financial management. Domain-specific organization finance may follow that domain.

## `transport`

Roads, bridges, sidewalks, cycling, parking, traffic, public/shared transport.

## `infrastructure`

Water, sewerage, WWTP, energy, heating, public lighting, telecom/technical networks.

## `planning`

Spatial planning only: zoning/land-use plan, its amendments, UAP and statutory spatial-planning processes.

## `strategy`

Cross-cutting municipal/territorial development strategies and action plans, including ITI strategic/territorial framework documents. Domain-specific strategies stay in their domain.

## `environment`

Nature, greenery, trees, environmental protection and flood-protection policy/projects.

## `waste`

Waste collection, recycling, collection yards, waste-management systems.

## `education`

Schools, kindergartens, school canteens and education.

## `culture`

Museums, libraries, cultural institutions/events, tourism information and heritage/culture.

## `sport`

Sports clubs, facilities, sporting events and recreation primarily sporting in nature.

## `social`

Social/health services, seniors, family policy and related support.

## `security`

Municipal police, JSDH/fire protection, emergency readiness and crime prevention. Cybersecurity remains administration.

## `administration`

General governance, internal administration, legal/process matters, generic office/IT/services and non-domain-specific municipal operations.

## Boundary rules

- A lease/easement/property disposal remains property even if the property will serve another domain.
- A repair/operation/procurement for a clearly domain-specific facility follows that domain.
- A domain-specific grant follows the substantive domain, not finance.
- planning is reserved for statutory spatial planning; strategic development documents use strategy.
- security covers public safety/emergency services, not cybersecurity.
- Exclude examples where the substantive purpose cannot be established from the text itself.
