# Data license

Licensing of the dataset is separated from licensing of the source code and
model weights.

## Project-created annotations and metadata

To the extent that the project contributors hold copyright or database rights
in the project-created annotations and metadata, including category labels,
curation decisions, exclusion reasons and taxonomy metadata, those contributions
are made available under:

**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

## Source resolution text

The JSONL dataset also contains text copied or extracted from public municipal
resolutions and related public documents.

Those source texts are **not relicensed by this repository**. Their inclusion in
the dataset does not assert ownership over them and does not change whatever
copyright, public-document or other legal status applies to the originals.

Users who redistribute or reuse the source text are responsible for determining
the legal basis applicable to that source material.

## Model outputs

Predictions, scores and other automatically generated inference metadata are not
part of the gold annotation license merely because they appear next to project
metadata.

This file is intended to make the repository's licensing boundaries explicit;
it is not legal advice.
